HDMI No Sound in Windows 11? How To Fix Audio Output

HDMI audio problems can be confusing because the video side often works perfectly. If the picture is on the TV or monitor but the sound is missing, that usually means the display path is good enough for video while the audio endpoint, speaker setup, driver, or adapter path still is not right.

Quick answer

Check whether Windows is sending audio to the correct display device

Quick answer

If HDMI video works but there is no sound in Windows 11, first select the HDMI display as the output device, confirm the TV or monitor actually has speakers, and check its volume before blaming the cable or driver.

Safe order

Best troubleshooting order for HDMI audio problems

  1. Select the correct output device in Windows. HDMI audio may not switch automatically.
  2. Check the TV or monitor volume. Also confirm it is not muted internally.
  3. Confirm the display actually has speakers or an audio path. Some monitors accept HDMI without playing sound.
  4. Reconnect the HDMI cable and restart the display if needed.
  5. Try another HDMI port or cable.
  6. Check Device Manager and the graphics audio device.
  7. Update the GPU and HDMI audio driver path if sound broke after changes.
  8. Test around docks, USB-C hubs, or adapters. These often change the behavior.

Monitor reality check

Some monitors take HDMI video but still cannot play audio

This catches people all the time. Windows may happily show the monitor as an HDMI audio device even if the monitor has no built-in speakers, no headphone output, or speakers that were disabled in the monitor menu. If you are using a desktop monitor instead of a TV, check the model specs before spending an hour on drivers.

If the broader issue is that Windows has no sound through any path, step back to No Sound in Windows 11. This page is specifically for the case where the video already works through HDMI.

Graphics audio path

HDMI audio often depends on the graphics device, not the regular speaker driver

HDMI and DisplayPort audio usually travel through the graphics hardware. That means NVIDIA, AMD, Intel graphics, or dock-related audio components may be involved even though you are troubleshooting sound. If Device Manager shows the display audio device missing or warning-marked, the graphics-side driver matters more than the usual laptop speaker driver.

Use How To Update Graphics Driver Windows 11 or the broader How To Update Drivers Windows 11 guide if the issue started after an update or docking change.

Adapters and docks

Docks, USB-C hubs, and adapters complicate HDMI audio

An HDMI cable by itself is one thing. A laptop running through USB-C, Thunderbolt, a dock, or a small HDMI adapter adds more variables. Some adapters handle video reliably while audio pass-through is inconsistent. If sound returns when you connect directly instead of through the dock, that tells you where the real fault probably lives.

If a dock or adapter also causes USB devices to misbehave, the issue may be broader than HDMI audio alone.

Display mode

Duplicate and extend mode can affect which audio endpoint stays active

Display mode is not usually the first thing to blame, but it can matter when several monitors are attached. In duplicate mode, Windows may behave differently about the active audio endpoint than it does in extend mode. If the sound device list keeps shifting as displays wake and sleep, test with only one external display connected.

Hardware signs

When HDMI audio trouble is more likely physical

  • Multiple HDMI cables and ports all fail with the same PC but work with another device
  • The dock or adapter gets unusually hot or behaves inconsistently
  • The TV or monitor audio works from another source device but never from this computer
  • The HDMI connector is visibly loose, damaged, or intermittent
  • Sound returns only when the cable is held at a certain angle

FAQ

Why do I get picture over HDMI but no sound in Windows 11?

This usually happens because Windows is using the wrong output device, the TV or monitor volume is off, the display has no built-in speakers, the graphics audio device is disabled, or the HDMI path through a dock or adapter is not passing audio correctly.

Do all monitors support sound over HDMI?

No. Some monitors accept HDMI video but have no built-in speakers or audio output path, so Windows can send audio there even though nothing will actually play.

Can a graphics driver affect HDMI sound?

Yes. HDMI and DisplayPort audio usually ride through the graphics device, so a GPU or graphics-audio driver issue can stop sound even while video still works.

Do docks and HDMI adapters cause audio problems?

Yes. Docks, USB-C hubs, and adapters add another link in the chain and can affect whether audio is passed through correctly.

Does display mode matter for HDMI audio?

Sometimes. Duplicate and extend modes can affect which display Windows treats as the active audio endpoint, especially when several screens are attached.

When is HDMI no sound more likely a hardware problem?

Hardware is more likely when several cables and ports fail the same way, the TV or monitor audio works with other devices but never with this PC, or docks and adapters behave inconsistently.
