External Hard Drive Keeps Disconnecting in Windows?

When an external drive repeatedly reconnects, the safest priority is stability and file protection. The problem may be as simple as a cable, but disconnects during reads or copies can also be an early failure warning.

Physical checks

Eliminate the weak points in the connection

  1. Replace the cable. A cable can power a drive yet lose the data connection.
  2. Try a different direct USB port. Avoid hubs, docks, and adapters while testing.
  3. Check the connector fit. A connector that moves easily can interrupt the link.
  4. Check external power. Desktop drives and some enclosures need a reliable power adapter.

Testing another PC and port is useful because it changes several variables without altering the drive.

Windows checks

Check drivers and USB power behavior after the basics

Look in Device Manager for warnings around Disk drives and USB controllers. If the issue began after an update, a driver update or rollback may be relevant; see How To Update Drivers in Windows 11 for a safe order.

If disconnections happen after idle time or sleep, USB selective suspend and adapter power-management settings are worth reviewing. Change one setting at a time so you can tell what helped.

Heat and enclosure

Heat, enclosure faults, and drive health matter too

Let the drive cool and keep its vents clear. An enclosure that gets unusually hot, a drive that loses connection under load, or a connection that changes when the cable is touched can point to the enclosure or power path rather than Windows.

If the drive appears in Disk Management but still cannot be opened, move to External Hard Drive Not Accessible in Windows.

Data protection

When this is probably a hardware problem

Repeated disconnects on multiple computers, clicking, slow access, file-copy errors, and disappearing partitions are serious warning signs. Copy the most important accessible data first. Do not format or run aggressive repair commands just to make the symptom disappear.

FAQ

External hard drive disconnecting FAQ

Why does my external hard drive keep disconnecting?

A loose cable, weak USB port, hub or dock, insufficient power, power management, driver issue, enclosure problem, overheating, or failing drive can cause repeated disconnects.

Can USB power saving disconnect an external hard drive?

It can contribute, especially after sleep or during idle time. Check the physical connection first, then review USB power-management settings as a later step.

Should I keep using a drive that disconnects while copying?

If the data is important, copy the most important accessible files first and avoid repeated large transfers until the cause is clear.

Can an external drive enclosure cause disconnects?

Yes. A failing enclosure, its cable, or its power supply can make a healthy internal drive disconnect intermittently.
