Windows 11 Wi-Fi Keeps Disconnecting

Intermittent Wi-Fi problems are usually more frustrating than total failure because they come and go just enough to waste your time. On Windows 11, disconnects often show up after sleep, on battery power, after updates, or when the laptop moves farther from the router.

Quick answer

First check whether it is one laptop or the whole network

Quick answer

If only one Windows 11 laptop keeps disconnecting, power settings, drivers, or the Wi-Fi adapter are strong suspects. If several devices disconnect, look harder at the router, signal quality, or wireless interference.

Common causes

Common causes of repeated Wi-Fi disconnects

  • Weak signal or interference
  • Power-saving settings on the Wi-Fi adapter
  • Driver issues after updates or wake-from-sleep problems
  • Router instability or crowded wireless channels
  • Bad saved network profiles or roaming issues

A very common support pattern is a laptop that stays fine on AC power but drops Wi-Fi much more often on battery because power management gets too aggressive.

Troubleshooting flow

Step-by-step troubleshooting that starts with the most likely fixes

  1. Check whether other devices disconnect too.
  2. Forget and reconnect to the network. This clears a damaged saved Wi-Fi profile.
  3. Check Wi-Fi adapter power management. This matters a lot after sleep and on battery power.
  4. Check whether the timing points to a driver issue. If it started after Windows Update, rollback may help as much as updating.
  5. Test near the router. If the problem gets better up close, signal and interference deserve more attention.

If your Wi-Fi stays connected but websites still fail, switch to the Wi-Fi Connected But No Internet guide. If Windows shows no wireless networks at all, the No Wi-Fi Networks Found in Windows guide is the better fit.

When hardware may be involved

When the wireless adapter itself becomes suspicious

Hardware suspicion goes up when the adapter disappears from Windows, fails across different locations and routers, or behaves worse each week no matter what software settings you change. That is when a driver-only theory starts looking weak.

Professional help

When to stop troubleshooting on your own

Get professional help if the adapter keeps vanishing in Device Manager, the problem affects every network you try, or the router side is clearly healthy but the laptop still drops Wi-Fi constantly. That is when hardware diagnosis becomes more valuable than repeating resets.

FAQ

Why does Windows 11 Wi-Fi keep disconnecting?

Common reasons include signal weakness, Wi-Fi adapter power saving, router issues, outdated or unstable drivers, and changes that started after a Windows update.

Can power saving make Wi-Fi disconnect on Windows 11?

Yes. Power management can suspend or throttle the wireless adapter, especially on battery power or after the laptop wakes from sleep.

Should I update or roll back the Wi-Fi driver?

If disconnects began after an update or driver change, rollback can be just as important as updating. The timing matters more than blindly chasing a newer version.

How do I know if the router is the problem?

If multiple devices disconnect, the router, access point, or wireless environment is more likely. If only one Windows laptop disconnects, focus on the laptop first.

When is hardware more likely to be the cause?

Hardware becomes more likely when the Wi-Fi card disappears from Windows, fails across many networks, or disconnects no matter what settings or drivers you try.

When should I get professional help for Wi-Fi disconnects?

Get help if disconnects continue across multiple networks, the adapter keeps vanishing in Device Manager, or the issue returns even after driver and router checks.
