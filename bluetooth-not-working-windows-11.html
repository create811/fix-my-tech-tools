Bluetooth Not Working in Windows 11

Bluetooth problems in Windows 11 do not all mean the same thing. Sometimes the Bluetooth toggle disappears completely. Sometimes a mouse, headset, or speaker shows as paired but refuses to connect. In other cases Bluetooth vanished right after an update and the real issue is the adapter driver, not the accessory itself.

Quick answer

Start by confirming the PC actually has working Bluetooth hardware

Quick answer

If Bluetooth is missing in Windows 11, first confirm the PC has Bluetooth hardware, then check airplane mode, the Bluetooth toggle, and a full restart before moving into Device Manager, drivers, or services.

What to check first

Troubleshooting order for Bluetooth problems in Windows 11

  1. Confirm Bluetooth exists on the PC. Desktop towers and some older laptops do not always include built-in Bluetooth.
  2. Check airplane mode and the Bluetooth toggle. If airplane mode is on, wireless features may be suppressed together.
  3. Restart both Windows and the Bluetooth device. Headsets, keyboards, and mice can get stuck in a bad state.
  4. Remove the device and pair it again. This helps when the device shows as remembered but will not reconnect cleanly.
  5. Check Device Manager. A missing adapter, warning icon, or disabled device points toward the PC side of the problem.
  6. Check Windows Update and the laptop maker's driver page. This matters most if Bluetooth disappeared after an update.
  7. Check services only if needed. Services are not usually the first cause when the adapter itself is missing.
  8. Consider hardware failure or no built-in Bluetooth. If nothing Bluetooth-related exists anywhere, hardware may be disabled, absent, or failed.

Symptom guide

What the symptom usually tells you

A missing Bluetooth toggle usually points to the adapter, the driver, or a system-level setting. A paired device that will not connect is more often a pairing or profile problem. A device that works sometimes and drops after sleep can be tied to power management or a bad driver update.

Timing matters too. If Bluetooth disappeared right after Windows changed drivers in the background, that is different from a laptop that never showed Bluetooth in the first place. If the whole machine has also been unstable recently, the Laptop Keeps Freezing Randomly guide can help you decide whether Bluetooth is part of a broader hardware or driver problem.

Safe checks

Confirm Bluetooth hardware before chasing Windows settings

Open Device Manager and look for a Bluetooth category or a wireless adapter that includes Bluetooth. If you only see Wi-Fi and there is no Bluetooth entry at all, do not assume Windows hid it by accident. Some PCs simply do not include it, and some internal combo cards fail on the Bluetooth side while Wi-Fi keeps working.

If the PC recently updated and hardware seems present but unstable, the How To Update Drivers Windows 11 guide walks through the safest way to refresh the driver without using random updater software.

Pairing problems

When the device appears but will not connect

If your headphones, speaker, mouse, or keyboard appears in Bluetooth settings but stays stuck on paired or connected only briefly, remove it and pair it again. This matters because Windows can keep an old pairing record even after the accessory changed firmware, battery level, or connection state.

Also restart the accessory itself. That sounds obvious, but many Bluetooth problems are actually the accessory refusing a fresh handshake until it fully powers off and back on.

Device Manager

What to look for in Device Manager

In Device Manager, a downward arrow suggests the adapter is disabled. A yellow warning icon suggests a driver or hardware communication problem. If the Bluetooth category is missing entirely, check hidden devices and then compare what you see against the laptop model's hardware specs.

If Bluetooth stopped working right around a Windows patch cycle, also check whether the update process itself is still unfinished. The Windows 11 Update Stuck guide is useful when Windows is still sorting out follow-up updates or driver installs.

Drivers and services

Driver refresh first, services second

If the adapter is present but not working normally, refresh the Bluetooth driver from Windows Update or the laptop manufacturer's support page. Laptop makers often package Wi-Fi and Bluetooth together because they share the same wireless card.

Only after the adapter is confirmed present does it make sense to look at Bluetooth services. If the adapter is missing at the hardware or driver level, restarting services will not bring back a device Windows cannot see.

What not to do

Common Bluetooth troubleshooting mistakes

  • Assuming every PC has built-in Bluetooth without checking the hardware first
  • Installing random driver updater tools instead of using Windows Update or the manufacturer page
  • Restarting services before confirming the Bluetooth adapter exists in Device Manager
  • Blaming the accessory when the Bluetooth toggle itself is gone from Windows
  • Re-pairing the same device repeatedly without restarting the PC or the accessory

Hardware warning

When Bluetooth may be a hardware problem

If Bluetooth disappeared after a drop, repair, or liquid spill, or if Device Manager never shows a Bluetooth adapter at all, hardware becomes more likely. On some laptops Bluetooth can also be disabled in BIOS or UEFI, especially after a firmware reset. If the PC has no practical built-in Bluetooth path, a USB Bluetooth adapter is often the simplest workaround.

FAQ

Why did Bluetooth disappear from Windows 11?

Bluetooth can disappear when the adapter is disabled, the driver is damaged, airplane mode is interfering, Windows Update changed the driver, or the PC does not actually have working Bluetooth hardware.

How do I know whether my PC has Bluetooth hardware?

Check Device Manager for a Bluetooth category or a wireless adapter that includes Bluetooth. If nothing Bluetooth-related appears even after showing hidden devices and checking the manufacturer specs, the PC may not have built-in Bluetooth.

Should I reinstall the Bluetooth device before updating drivers?

Not first. Start with simple checks like airplane mode, the Bluetooth toggle, a restart, and removing and pairing the device again. Reinstalling or refreshing drivers makes more sense after those basics fail.

Can Windows Update break Bluetooth?

Yes, sometimes. A Windows update can change the Bluetooth driver or power behavior, which is why it helps to check both Windows Update history and the laptop maker's support page if Bluetooth vanished after an update.

When should I check Bluetooth services in Windows 11?

Check services only after confirming the PC has Bluetooth hardware and the adapter is present in Device Manager. Services are rarely the first thing to blame when the toggle is missing entirely.

What if my laptop never had reliable Bluetooth?

If the laptop never had built-in Bluetooth, or the internal adapter has failed, a small USB Bluetooth adapter can be a practical solution instead of fighting missing hardware.
